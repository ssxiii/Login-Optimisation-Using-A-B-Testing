# Visualisation Guide – Login Optimisation

## 1. Objective

Create visualisations to compare the performance of Group A (existing email/password login) and Group B (new mobile number + OTP login).

The charts will help communicate experiment results and support business decisions.

## 2. Planned Visualisations

### 1. Login Success Rate Comparison

**Chart Type:** Bar Chart

Compare login success rates between Group A and Group B.

**Purpose:**
- Identify which login experience performs better.
- Highlight the difference in success rates.

### 2. Login Failure and Abandonment Rate

**Chart Type:** Clustered Bar Chart

Compare login failure and abandonment rates across both groups.

**Purpose:**
- Identify where users experience login difficulties.
- Understand whether OTP authentication reduces login friction.

### 3. Login Completion Time

**Chart Type:** Bar Chart

Compare average login completion time between the two groups.

**Purpose:**
- Evaluate which authentication method provides a faster experience.

### 4. Conversion Lift

**Chart Type:** KPI Cards

Display:
- Group A login success rate.
- Group B login success rate.
- Absolute lift.
- Relative lift.

**Purpose:**
Provide a quick overview of the experiment's primary outcome.

### 5. Confidence Interval

**Chart Type:** Error Bar Chart

Display the estimated difference in login success rates and its 95% confidence interval.

**Purpose:**
- Communicate the estimated treatment effect.
- Show the uncertainty around the result.
- Identify whether the interval includes zero.

## 3. Tools

- Microsoft Excel
- Google Sheets

## 4. Visualisation Guidelines

- Use consistent group labels and metric definitions.
- Display percentages with appropriate precision.
- Clearly distinguish percentage points from relative percentage change.
- Include chart titles and axis labels.
- Avoid misleading axis scales.
- Clearly identify synthetic practice results.

## 5. Expected Outcome

Create clear, stakeholder-friendly visualisations that communicate experiment performance, statistical findings and potential business impact.
